Overview

Lighting & Fan Upgrades for your home

Lighting and air movement shape how every room feels. Well-placed lighting makes kitchens more functional and living spaces more inviting, while ceiling fans and exhaust fans help keep Florida homes comfortable.

Bright Flow Services installs and upgrades lighting and fans throughout the home, from recessed LED lighting and dimmer controls to landscape lighting, security lighting, ceiling fans, and bathroom exhaust fans. We help plan placement and controls so the result works the way you live.

Benefits

Why homeowners choose lighting & fan upgrades

Better light where you need it

Layered lighting improves visibility for tasks and creates a more inviting atmosphere.

Energy-efficient LED

LED fixtures use less energy than older incandescent lighting and typically last much longer.

Comfort in Florida’s climate

Ceiling fans help circulate air, and exhaust fans help remove bathroom humidity.

Security & curb appeal

Exterior and landscape lighting helps light entries, walkways, and yard areas.

When to call

Signs it may be time to talk to an electrician

These are common reasons homeowners reach out. If any apply to you, we’ll help you understand your options.

  • 1Rooms feel dark or depend on lamps
  • 2Fixtures flicker, buzz, or look dated
  • 3You want to adjust brightness with dimmers
  • 4A ceiling fan wobbles, or you want fans in more rooms
  • 5Your bathroom stays humid or shows signs of mildew
  • 6Walkways, entries, or the yard are poorly lit at night

Safety first. If you notice a burning smell, sparking, scorch marks, or a panel, outlet, or switch that feels hot, stop using the affected circuit if it’s safe to do so and contact a qualified electrician. If there is smoke or fire, leave the building and call 911.

FAQ

Lighting & Fan Upgrades questions

Can recessed lighting be added to an existing ceiling?

Often, yes. Remodel-style fixtures are designed for existing ceilings. Access, insulation, and framing all affect the layout, which we review during planning.

Will my LED lights work with a dimmer?

Only if the bulbs or fixtures and the dimmer are designed to work together. Mismatched components can flicker or buzz, so we help select compatible combinations.

Can a ceiling fan go where a light fixture is now?

Frequently, but a ceiling fan needs an electrical box and support rated for a fan. We check the existing box and support and replace them if needed.

Why does my bathroom need an exhaust fan?

Exhaust fans help remove moisture from showers and baths. In a humid climate like Florida’s, managing moisture helps protect finishes and reduces conditions that encourage mildew.

Do you install landscape lighting?

Yes. We install landscape and pathway lighting and can help plan placement, controls such as timers or photocells, and how the system is powered.
