Overview

Specialized Equipment Hookups for Florida businesses

Specialized commercial equipment often has demanding electrical requirements: specific voltages, single- or three-phase power, dedicated circuits, disconnects, and protection sized to the load. Getting those details right affects performance, equipment life, and safety.

Bright Flow Services provides electrical connections for commercial kitchen equipment, large HVAC units, industrial pumps, and other specialized equipment. We review nameplate data and manufacturer installation requirements, confirm your available capacity, and coordinate with equipment vendors and mechanical contractors so installations come together smoothly.

When to call

When to bring us in

Common situations where business owners, property managers, and contractors reach out.

  • 1You’re opening or renovating a commercial kitchen
  • 2You’re replacing or adding rooftop or large HVAC equipment
  • 3You’re installing pumps, compressors, or production equipment
  • 4New equipment requires three-phase power or a different voltage
  • 5Equipment trips breakers or isn’t performing as expected

Safety first. If equipment is smoking, sparking, or overheating, or a panel shows signs of burning, keep people away from it and call 911 if there is any fire. Don’t open electrical panels or repeatedly reset breakers; contact a qualified electrician to investigate.

FAQ

Specialized Equipment Hookups questions

What information do you need about our equipment?

Manufacturer, model, nameplate data or cut sheets, and installation manuals are the most helpful. They tell us the voltage, phase, current, and connection requirements.

Can you tell us whether our building can support new equipment?

Yes. We assess your existing service and distribution to determine whether there’s capacity for the new load and what changes may be needed if there isn’t.

Do you coordinate with our equipment supplier or mechanical contractor?

Yes. Equipment installations usually involve several parties, and we coordinate scope and timing with suppliers, installers, and mechanical contractors.

What if new equipment needs three-phase power and we only have single-phase?

Options depend on your site and utility. We can evaluate whether a commercial service upgrade is feasible or whether alternative equipment configurations make more sense.

Can you relocate existing equipment connections?

Yes. If you’re rearranging a kitchen, production area, or mechanical space, we can plan and relocate electrical connections to suit the new layout.
