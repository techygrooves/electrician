Who we are

Electrical work done with care, for Florida homes and businesses

Bright Flow Services provides residential and commercial electrical services in Florida. We work with homeowners, business owners, property managers, and general contractors on projects that range from electrical panel upgrades and EV charger installation to emergency electrical repairs and commercial lighting retrofits.

Work is led by a journeyman-qualified electrician with OSHA 10 and OSHA 30 safety training and CPR certification, so safety is built into how every job is planned and carried out.

Our approach is straightforward: understand what you need, explain your options in plain language, put the scope in writing, and complete the work carefully. The goal is an installation that is safe, organized, and right for how you use it.
