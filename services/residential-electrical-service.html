When to call

Signs it may be time to talk to an electrician

These are common reasons homeowners reach out. If any apply to you, we’ll help you understand your options.

  • 1A breaker trips repeatedly or won’t reset
  • 2Part of your home has lost power
  • 3An outlet or switch is warm, sparking, or discolored
  • 4Lights flicker or dim without an obvious cause
  • 5There’s a burning smell near an outlet, switch, or panel
  • 6Outlets, switches, or fixtures have stopped working

Safety first. If there is smoke, sparking, or fire, leave the home and call 911. Otherwise, stop using the affected outlet or appliance, and don’t keep resetting a breaker that trips. Call a qualified electrician to investigate.

FAQ

Questions about residential service & repairs

Do you handle electrical emergencies?

Yes. We provide both emergency and non-emergency residential electrical service. For an urgent problem, call (321) 442-9943 to describe what’s happening and discuss the earliest available time. If there is smoke, fire, or immediate danger, call 911 first.

What counts as an electrical emergency?

A burning smell, smoke, sparking, an outlet or panel that is hot or discolored, or a loss of power to important parts of your home should be treated as urgent. A single dead outlet or occasional flicker is usually not an emergency, but it is still worth having checked.

Why does my breaker keep tripping?

Common causes include an overloaded circuit, a short circuit, a ground fault, a failing appliance, or a worn breaker. Repeated tripping is a warning sign, so it should be investigated rather than reset again and again.

Can you fix a single outlet or switch?

Yes. Small repairs are part of residential service work. While we’re there, we can also check whether the problem points to a larger issue on that circuit.

Is it safe to make my own electrical repairs?

We advise against it. Electrical repairs carry real risks of shock and fire, and the cause of a problem is not always where it appears to be. Have repairs made by a qualified electrician.
