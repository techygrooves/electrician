Overview

New Construction Wiring for Florida businesses

New construction is the best opportunity to get a building’s electrical system right. Decisions made during design and rough-in determine how well a space supports tenants, equipment, and future changes for years to come.

Bright Flow Services provides electrical installations for new offices, retail spaces, and multi-family buildings, including power distribution, lighting, and appropriate data infrastructure. We work from your drawings and specifications, coordinate with the general contractor and other trades, and keep the electrical scope moving in step with the overall schedule.

Tell us about your project

What this service can include

  • Service entrance, distribution, and panels per the project design
  • Branch circuits and receptacles for tenant and common areas
  • Interior, exterior, and site lighting with controls
  • Low-voltage pathways and data cabling infrastructure as specified
  • Power connections for HVAC and building systems
  • Multi-family unit wiring, metering, and common-area power

Common applications

  • Office buildings
  • Retail spaces
  • Multi-family buildings
  • Mixed-use developments

FAQ

New Construction Wiring questions

Do you work directly with general contractors?

Yes. We can work as the electrical trade under a general contractor or directly with an owner or developer, depending on how your project is structured.

Can you price a project from drawings?

Yes. Share your plans and specifications and we’ll review the electrical scope and provide a written proposal. Complete drawings and a defined scope lead to the most accurate proposals.

Do you install data and low-voltage infrastructure?

We can include data infrastructure in the electrical scope, such as pathways, boxes, and cabling, as appropriate for the project. Let us know your requirements and any IT vendors we’ll be coordinating with.

What information do you need to get started?

Project location, drawings or a scope description, the schedule, and key contacts. For multi-family and larger projects, specifications and any utility requirements help us scope accurately.

Can you suggest value-engineering options?

Where it makes sense, we can suggest alternative approaches or materials that still meet the project’s requirements. Any changes are reviewed with the design team and owner before they’re adopted.
