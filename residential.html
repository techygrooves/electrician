When to call

Signs your home’s electrical system needs attention

Electrical issues rarely fix themselves. If any of these sound familiar, it’s a good time to have your system evaluated.

  • 1Breakers trip often or lights flicker and dim
  • 2Outlets or switches feel warm, spark, or stop working
  • 3Your home relies on extension cords and power strips
  • 4You’re adding an EV, hot tub, generator, or major appliance
  • 5Your panel or wiring is original to an older home
  • 6You’re planning a renovation, addition, or smart home upgrade

Safety first. If you notice a burning smell, sparking, scorch marks, or a panel, outlet, or switch that feels hot, stop using the affected circuit if it’s safe to do so and contact a qualified electrician. If there is smoke or fire, leave the building and call 911.

FAQ

Residential electrical questions

What residential electrical services does Bright Flow Services offer?

We provide electrical panel upgrades, EV charger installation, backup generator installation, lighting and fan upgrades, dedicated electrical circuits, and smart home integration, along with electrical troubleshooting. Each service has its own page with more detail.

How do I request an estimate?

Use our contact page to share a few details about your home and project. We’ll follow up to discuss your needs, review the site or system when needed, and provide a written proposal before any work begins.

How do I know whether my home needs an electrical upgrade?

Frequent breaker trips, dimming lights, warm outlets, heavy reliance on extension cords, and plans for major new equipment are all common reasons to have your system evaluated. We’ll review your home and explain what, if anything, makes sense.

Is it safe to do my own electrical work?

Electrical work carries real risks of shock, fire, and serious injury, and mistakes are not always obvious until something goes wrong. We recommend having electrical work in your home performed by a qualified electrician.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve homeowners in Florida. Contact us with your address and project details and we’ll confirm availability for your location.

Planning electrical work?

Tell us about your home or business project and we’ll follow up to talk through the details and next steps.
