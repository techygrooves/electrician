Commercial electrical contracting

Electrical work that supports the way your business runs

For a business, electrical work is never just about wiring. It affects opening dates, tenant satisfaction, equipment uptime, energy costs, and the safety of everyone in the building. The right electrical partner understands those stakes and plans accordingly.

Bright Flow Services delivers commercial electrical services across Florida, from new construction wiring and LED lighting retrofits to specialized equipment hookups, service and load upgrades, and troubleshooting. Every engagement starts with understanding your operation, then defining a clear, written scope, so decision-makers know exactly what’s being delivered, when, and why.

Project workflow

FAQ

Commercial electrical questions

What types of commercial properties do you work on?

We provide electrical services for offices, retail stores, restaurants, multi-family buildings, warehouses, and other commercial facilities in Florida. Contact us to discuss your property and project.

Do you work with property managers and general contractors?

Yes. We work with business owners, property managers, general contractors, and facility teams, and adapt communication and scheduling to how each project is run.

Can work be scheduled around our business operations?

We plan work to limit disruption and will discuss scheduling options that fit your operation, including phasing work by area and planning any required shutdowns in advance.

How do you price commercial projects?

Pricing depends on scope, site conditions, materials, and schedule. After reviewing your project, we provide a written proposal with the scope and any assumptions clearly stated.

What should we include in a project enquiry?

Property type and location, a description of the work, your timeline, and any drawings, specifications, or equipment information you have. The more detail you share, the more accurate our proposal can be.

Do you handle urgent electrical problems?

We diagnose and repair commercial electrical faults, including tripping breakers, failed panels, and loss of power. Contact us to describe the issue and discuss availability. If there is smoke, fire, or immediate danger, call 911.
