Overview

Commercial Service & Load Upgrades for Florida businesses

As businesses grow, electrical demand grows with them: more equipment, more HVAC, EV charging, expanded kitchens or production lines that need specialized equipment hookups. When demand outpaces your service, the symptoms range from nuisance trips to equipment that simply can’t be added.

Bright Flow Services performs electrical service assessments and capacity expansions for commercial properties. We evaluate your existing service, distribution, and loads, identify constraints, and recommend practical upgrades, from additional panels and distribution to a larger service coordinated with your utility when required.

Tell us about your project

  • Assessment of existing service, switchgear, panels, and distribution
  • Load evaluation based on current and planned equipment
  • Service capacity upgrades and new distribution equipment
  • Additional panels and subpanels for expanded areas
  • Capacity planning for EV charging, HVAC, and equipment additions
  • Planning for utility coordination on service changes

FAQ

Commercial Service & Load Upgrades questions

What does an electrical service assessment include?

We review your service size, main distribution, panels, and existing loads, along with the equipment you plan to add. The goal is a clear picture of available capacity and any constraints.

Will a service upgrade require shutting off power?

Most service upgrades require at least one planned outage. We work with you to schedule shutdowns at times that limit the impact on your business and coordinate with the utility when its involvement is required.

How long does a commercial service upgrade take?

Timelines depend on the scope, equipment lead times, and utility scheduling. Your proposal will outline the expected sequence so you can plan around it.

Do we always need a bigger service?

Not necessarily. Sometimes better distribution, load balancing, or additional panels solve the problem. We’ll explain which options fit your situation.

Can you plan for EV charging at our property?

Yes. EV charging can add significant load, so we evaluate capacity first and recommend an approach that fits your building and the number of chargers you plan to install.
