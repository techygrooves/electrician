Overview

Commercial Lighting Retrofits for Florida businesses

Lighting is one of the most visible parts of any commercial space and often one of its larger energy loads. Aging fluorescent and incandescent systems use more power, fail more often, and can leave spaces dim, uneven, or flickering. (If flickering is affecting a whole building, electrical troubleshooting may be the first step.)

Bright Flow Services replaces outdated fluorescent and incandescent lighting with energy-efficient LED systems. We assess your existing fixtures and how each area is used, then recommend the right approach, from retrofit kits in existing housings to full fixture replacement and updated controls.

Benefits

Business benefits of commercial lighting retrofits

Lower energy use

LED lighting typically uses significantly less energy than fluorescent and incandescent systems.

Less maintenance

Longer-lasting LED sources mean fewer lamp replacements and service calls.

Better light quality

More uniform light and better color can improve visibility, comfort, and presentation.

Smarter control

Sensors and schedules help keep lights off in unoccupied areas.

FAQ

Commercial Lighting Retrofits questions

Should we use retrofit kits or replace fixtures entirely?

It depends on the condition of the existing housings, the ceiling type, and your goals. Retrofit kits can be cost-effective when fixtures are in good shape, while full replacement may make more sense for damaged, outdated, or poorly performing fixtures.

Can a retrofit be done without closing our business?

Often, yes. Retrofits can usually be phased by area, and we’ll discuss scheduling options that fit your operation.

What happens to the old lamps and ballasts?

Some fluorescent lamps contain mercury and some older ballasts require special handling. We’ll discuss removal and disposal as part of the project scope.

Are utility rebates available for LED upgrades?

Some Florida utilities have offered incentives for qualifying commercial lighting upgrades. Programs change, so check with your utility for current availability and requirements.

Will LED lighting work with our existing controls?

Some controls are compatible and some aren’t. Older dimmers and lighting controls may not work properly with LED drivers, so we review your existing controls and recommend compatible options.
